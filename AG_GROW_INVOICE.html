PROJECT SUMMARY

A complete online order management system built for AGRO PRODUCTS. The system allows Customers to place orders (AG GROW or Sulfag product sheets), Head Office to review and forward to Admin, Admin to approve and send to the Factory, and the Factory to enter availability values — all through a single web app branded as the AGRO PRODUCTS Order Portal. Includes in-app notifications at every step.
